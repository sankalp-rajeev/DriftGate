# D7 offline fault scoring pre-registration, 26 Nov 2026

This protocol supersedes the [earlier D7 plan](2026-11-26-d7.md). The
[interrupted regeneration](../deviations/2026-11-26-d7-interrupted-regeneration.md)
is excluded from calibration and all fault results. This note records the fitted
thresholds before any archived fault continuations are scored with D7.

The reference is the seed-0 measurement for the baseline in `gate/baseline.yaml`.
Its measurement SHA-256 is
`20f0a6fe2f3e2557195474e3494bf384e50194cd2adeefbb9ae92b6bd63b19e6`.
Use the 19–20 Nov laptop study's `aa/run-00` through `aa/run-19` for fitting and
`aa/run-20` through `aa/run-29` for the held-out check. Detokenize the archived D3
continuation IDs with the pinned reference tokenizer. Embed the texts using
the F16 BGE Small EN v1.5 GGUF pinned in `serving/models.yaml` and the standalone
`llama-embedding` executable from the pinned engine, with CLS pooling and L2
normalization. Identical text reuses the same vector. Pair continuations by prompt
ID. Two blank continuations score 1, and a blank paired with a nonblank scores 0.
Report mean and linearly interpolated p5 cosine similarity. The statistic is
1 minus p5. Fit the maximum statistic across the 20 fit runs for each D3 prefix.
Detection requires a statistic strictly above the threshold. Archived request errors
count as detections in prefixes containing the failed request.

| Level | D3 prompts | Fitted upper threshold | Held-out false alarms | Exact 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| L1 | 16 | 0.0 | 0/10 | 0–30.85% |
| L2 | 32 | 0.0 | 0/10 | 0–30.85% |
| L3 | 64 | 0.0 | 0/10 | 0–30.85% |

The [calibration artifact](../../results/2026-11-19-study/d7-calibration.json) has SHA-256
`581eb555885c26ee5e8b50e1664e16c04ae5c649b38e29e0d3acaaa2ce0511f1`.
The exact interval is Clopper–Pearson. The maximum-of-20 rule gives a
per-detector bound of 1/21 under exchangeability.

Score the archived F1–F9 repetitions on all three prefixes. The main matrix
uses the corrected b12557 F8 pair. Score the archived v1.0.0 downgrade separately.
The [repair record](../results/f8-repair.json) identifies and pins both pairs.

D2, D3, D5 and D6 each have an implicit weight of 1.0 after threshold normalization.
D7's explicit weight of 1.0 follows the same convention. GateScore is the maximum
of the applicable detector scores and blocks above 1. Lab transfer, reference
upload, and deployment verification are deferred until after the Dec 2 teardown.
